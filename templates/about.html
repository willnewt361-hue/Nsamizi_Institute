About Nsamizi Training Institute

History & Mission

Founded in 1953, Nsamizi Training Institute of Social Development (NTISD) is a premier institution dedicated to training professional social development workers. Our mission is to equip students with knowledge, skills, and values needed to contribute to sustainable social development.

Academic Programs

We offer diverse academic programs tailored to meet the needs of social development professionals:

  • ✓ Social Work - Diploma & Degree
  • ✓ Community Health Management
  • ✓ Guidance and Counseling
  • ✓ Gender and Development
  • ✓ Business Administration
  • ✓ Human Resource Management

Community Engagement

NTISD is committed to community development and social responsibility. We maintain strategic partnerships with organizations like UNHCR, supporting refugee populations with essential services including water, sanitation, and sustainable livelihood programs.

Digital Fortress System

Our modern student management system ensures secure tracking of academic progress, transparent fee management, and efficient communication between students, lecturers, and administration. Built with enterprise-grade security and designed for accessibility across all our centers.
