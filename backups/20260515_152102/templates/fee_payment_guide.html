💳 Fee Payment Guide

Learn how to pay your tuition fees online or through various payment methods.

📱
Mobile Money
  1. Dial *165# on your MTN/Airtel number
  2. Select Send Money
  3. Enter recipient: +256 774 123 456
  4. Enter amount (tuition balance)
  5. Enter your PIN and confirm
  6. Screenshot or note transaction reference
Instant No fees
🏦
Bank Transfer
  1. Account: NTISD - Northern Technical and Social Institute
  2. Account #: 1234567890
  3. Bank: Kampala Doctors' Hospital
  4. Branch: Mpigi
  5. Include student reg # in reference
  6. Use online banking or visit branch
1-2 Days Bank fees apply
💰
Cash Payment
  1. Visit NTISD Main Campus - Mpigi
  2. Go to Finance Office
  3. Provide your student registration number
  4. Hand cash to cashier
  5. Request and keep receipt
  6. Bring receipt to class
Immediate No fees

⚠️ Important Information

  • Always include your registration number with all payments
  • Keep payment receipts for your records and dispute resolution
  • Contact admin if payment not reflected within 48 hours
  • Semester 1: January - May | Semester 2: August - December
  • Contact Finance Office: +256 393 123 456 for payment status inquiries
  • Overdue payments may result in suspension of services
